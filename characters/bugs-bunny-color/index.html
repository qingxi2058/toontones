Why it works

Low saturation is unforgiving

Small saturation changes are easier to notice when the color is close to gray.

Warmth changes the feeling

Warm gray feels soft; cool gray feels metallic.

Soft pink anchors the set

A gentle accent color makes the neutral shades easier to compare.

How to practice neutral cartoon colors

Gray and cream pages are useful because they remove the easy part of color guessing. You cannot rely on a bright hue label. Instead, you have to judge whether the shade is warmer, cooler, lighter, or more muted than your first memory.

Set saturation lower than feels natural, then use lightness to shape the final guess. If the color looks dirty, the hue may be too warm. If it looks metallic, it may be too cool. Repeat the palette after a colorful round to train subtle contrast.
