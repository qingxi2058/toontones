Why Character Colors Are Harder Than They Look

You have seen these characters a thousand times, so you assume you know their colors. But ask someone to set SpongeBob yellow or Mario red on a blank slider with no reference, and most people miss. That gap between recognition and recall is what ToonTones measures. Each round shows a shade, hides it, and scores your reproduction 0–10.

Character Colors FAQ

What are cartoon character colors?

Cartoon character colors are the signature hex codes that define iconic characters — like SpongeBob yellow (#F9E03B) or Mario red (#E52521). ToonTones turns them into a color memory game.

Can I test if I know these colors?

Yes. ToonTones shows a character shade, hides it, and scores how accurately you rebuild it from memory on a 0–10 scale.
