SpongeBob Yellow Hex Code

The classic SpongeBob body color is a warm, slightly green-leaning yellow. The single value most sources settle on is #F9E03B (RGB 249, 224, 59). Because the character appears across the TV animation, logos, merchandise, and fan recreations, you will see a few close variants in the wild:

SpongeBob Yellow

Which one looks "right" partly depends on your screen. An uncalibrated monitor shifts yellows noticeably, which is also why reproducing a remembered shade is harder than it sounds.

SpongeBob Color FAQ

What color is SpongeBob?

SpongeBob SquarePants is bright yellow. The most commonly used SpongeBob yellow hex code is #F9E03B, with #FFF359 also widely cited depending on the source and screen.

What is the SpongeBob yellow hex code?

The SpongeBob yellow hex code is #F9E03B (RGB 249, 224, 59). Alternate values seen across merchandise and logos include #FFF359 and #F9E24D.

What are the other colors in SpongeBob's design?

Beyond his yellow body, SpongeBob wears a white shirt, a red tie (about #D62828), brown shorts (about #A56E3A), and has blue eyes (about #4FB0E5).
