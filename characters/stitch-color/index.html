Why this shade is tricky

Blue shifts fast

A small hue change can make the color feel too cyan or too purple.

Brightness matters

Light blues feel friendly; darker blues feel more intense and game-like.

Memory fills gaps

Most players remember "blue" but not the exact warmth of the blue.

How to use this palette

Study the main blue first, then compare it with the lighter teal and darker blue swatches. The goal is not to copy an official image; it is to train the difference between a remembered character color and the exact shade you can rebuild with sliders.

For a better score, avoid moving saturation too high. Many blue guesses look exciting but become too neon. Lock the hue, lower saturation slightly, then adjust lightness until the shade feels close to the one you studied.
