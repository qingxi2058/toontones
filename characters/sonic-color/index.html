Practice tips

Find blue first

Set the hue before chasing brightness.

Use red as contrast

Red makes the blue feel stronger, so judge them separately.

Do not over-brighten

Many guesses lose the rich game-blue feeling by raising lightness too far.

How to practice fast game blues

Game-style blue is memorable because it is bold, but that confidence can hurt the score. Players often jump straight to a bright saturated blue and miss the darker or warmer version they actually saw.

Use the darker blue swatch as your anchor. If your first guess looks too electric, lower lightness before changing hue. If it looks purple, move the hue back toward cyan only a little; big moves usually overshoot into a different blue family.
