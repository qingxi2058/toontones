How to practice classic cartoon colors

Classic cartoon palettes are useful because they mix simple labels with difficult execution. Red, black, yellow, and cream sound easy, but each one still has a specific warmth and brightness that affects the final ToonTones score.

Try matching red first, then switch to the cream shade. Moving between a loud color and a soft color trains your eye to reset instead of carrying the previous shade into the next guess.
