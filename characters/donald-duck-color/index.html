Why this page helps

Blue needs depth

A little darkness keeps the blue from turning flat.

Yellow needs warmth

Warm yellow feels more natural than pure digital yellow.

White changes contrast

White areas make nearby colors feel more saturated.

How to practice blue, yellow, and white

This palette is useful because the white swatch changes how the surrounding colors feel. Blue can appear more saturated next to white, while yellow can feel warmer than it actually is. That contrast makes the page a good training set for careful observation.

Start with the blue, then reset your eye before trying yellow. If you score poorly, check whether the miss came from hue or lightness. Many players remember the right color family but make the shade too bright after seeing white nearby.
