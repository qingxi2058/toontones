How game colors affect your score

Video game palettes are built for quick recognition. That helps players identify the broad color family, but it can hide small mistakes in hue, saturation, and lightness. ToonTones turns those small differences into a score you can improve round by round.

Reds usually fail when they drift toward orange or magenta. Blues fail when they become too cyan, too purple, or too bright. Yellows fail when they lose warmth and become flat digital yellow. Treat each shade as a measured color rather than a logo memory.

For the best practice loop, play one game-style page, check your score, then open the score guide to understand where the miss happened. That keeps the page useful even when a single round feels easy.
