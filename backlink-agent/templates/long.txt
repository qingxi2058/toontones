ToonTones is a free browser-based color memory game that challenges players to remember and match colors with increasing precision.

Key features:
- Color Memory Test: Discover how well you recall colors across difficulty levels
- Daily Color Challenge: A new challenge every day — compete and track improvement
- Color Accuracy Test: Measure how precisely you perceive color differences
- No signup, no install — plays instantly in any browser
- Works on mobile and desktop

Who is it for:
- Anyone curious about their color perception abilities
- Artists, designers, and photographers who want to train color sensitivity
- Casual gamers looking for a quick daily brain workout
- People who enjoy Wordle-style daily challenges
