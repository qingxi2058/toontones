What makes anime colors different

Anime-style palettes often use cleaner edges between color families than real-world scenes. Hair, outfit, aura, and mascot colors are designed to be recognized quickly, but that does not mean the exact shade is easy to rebuild from memory.

In ToonTones, the challenge is to separate the memory label from the actual color. "Orange outfit" or "yellow mascot" is only the starting point. The score improves when you can remember whether the shade leaned warmer, darker, softer, or more saturated than the simple label suggests.

Use this page when you want high-saturation practice before a full round. If your guesses look too neon, lower saturation first; if they look dull, check whether lightness is doing the damage before changing hue.
